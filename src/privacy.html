Privacy notice

Last updated 9 October 2026

The Starred Bill (starredbill.com) compares prices at Michelin-starred restaurants. This page explains what we keep about you, and why. You can use the whole site without an account.

Who we are

The Starred Bill is an independent website run from the United Kingdom. It isn't connected with Michelin or the MICHELIN Guide. We decide how the personal data described here is used, which makes us its “controller” under UK data protection law. For anything on this page, email hello@starredbill.com.

Without an account

Your wishlist and your choice of language and currency are kept only in your own browser (its local storage). They never reach us. We don't use advertising cookies.

To show the site, your browser also fetches things from other companies, and like any website they receive your IP address: Google (maps, restaurant photos and the site's fonts), ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com, for today's exchange rates when you change currency) and, only when you sign in, jsDelivr (the sign-in code).

Your location

When you tap “Near me” or “Use my location”, your browser asks whether to share your location. If you allow it, the distances to restaurants are worked out on your device; your location is never sent to us or recorded in our visit statistics. If you type a town or postcode on the Near me page instead, it's looked up with Google's place search. If you ask for travel times, or for the restaurants along a route, your starting point and the places you're going to are sent to Google to work them out. Only if you tick “Remember this location” is it saved, in your own browser, along with the stars of the restaurants nearby so the page can show which have won a star since your last visit; unticking the box removes both.

Cookies and Google Analytics

If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to recognise returning visitors and shows us how the site is used: pages viewed, time spent, where visitors come from, and the same anonymous clicks Umami counts. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. If you click Reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics doesn't run and sets no cookies.

Visit statistics

We count visits with Umami, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. It records the page, the site you came from, your country and type of device, and anonymous counts of clicks such as saving a restaurant, saving a diary entry (never what's in it), sharing a page, searching, showing a page's full list of restaurants or opening a restaurant's website. On Help me pick it counts the answers chosen, such as the city, meal or budget, but never your location. When someone creates an account or signs in, it records only that this happened and whether by email or Google, never your email address. When a member saves a preference, it records only which kind (home city, currency or dietary needs), never what was chosen. When a member turns star emails on or off, or unsubscribes, it records only that and which kind of email. Links in our star emails carry a tag saying they came from one, so we can count the visits they bring, never who opened them.

With a free account

When you create an account we keep:

  • your email address, and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture on your Google account
  • the restaurants on your wishlist and the ones you've marked as been there, with any dates you add
  • your preferences, only if you set them on Your account or say yes when we offer to remember a choice: your home city (one of the destinations on our site, such as London, never your address or location), the currency you want prices in, and your dietary needs
  • anything you write in your dining diary: what you paid per person and in which currency, the menu you had, and your notes on any saved restaurant
  • if you ask for star emails on Your account: which ones you chose (New stars near you, and the countries you want ceremony-night summaries for), a random code that makes the unsubscribe link in each email work, and a record of which of these emails we've sent you, so you never get one twice

Dietary needs such as halal, kosher or gluten-free can say something about your religion or health. We keep them only because you chose to save them, use them only to start Help me pick with that choice selected, and you can clear them at any time on Your account.

Your diary is private. No one else can see it, and we won't use it for anything, not even to check our prices, without asking you first.

We use these only to sign you in, to show your lists on any device, to start each page from your preferences and to send the star emails you asked for. We never sell them or share them for marketing, and we don't send newsletters unless you ask for them.

If you email us

We keep your email address and message so we can reply, and delete them once they're no longer needed (normally within a year).

Why we're allowed to use it

  • Your account: to provide the account you asked for (a contract with you).
  • Your dietary needs: your explicit consent, given when you choose to save them, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing them.
  • Star emails: your consent, given when you turn them on, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in any of them or on Your account.
  • Google Analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the Change cookie choice button above.
  • Umami visit counts and replying to emails: our legitimate interest in running and improving the site, in ways that don't identify you or that you started.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores accounts, lists and preferences, on servers in London.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails and the star emails you ask for.
  • Google, only if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Umami counts visits, without cookies (see Visit statistics).
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies.

Google, Resend and Umami may handle data outside the UK, including in the United States. When they do, they rely on the protections UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK's approved contract clauses.

Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it, along with the copy of your lists, diary and preferences on that device.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can do that at any time from Your account: it removes your account, both lists, your diary and your preferences straight away.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can see the data we hold, get a copy to take elsewhere (use Download my data on Your account), correct it, delete it, object to how it's used, or withdraw your consent. To ask about any of this, email hello@starredbill.com; we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

隱私權聲明

The Starred Bill（starredbill.com）比較米其林星級餐廳的價格。本頁說明我們會保存哪些關於你的資料，以及原因。不建立帳戶也能使用整個網站。

我們是誰

The Starred Bill 是在英國經營的獨立網站，與米其林及《米其林指南》沒有任何關係。本頁所述個人資料的使用方式由我們決定，因此依英國資料保護法，我們是這些資料的「控制者」。如對本頁有任何疑問，請寄信至 hello@starredbill.com。

未建立帳戶時

你的願望清單，以及語言與幣別設定，只會保存在你自己的瀏覽器（本機儲存空間）中，不會傳送給我們。我們不使用廣告 Cookie。

為了顯示網站，你的瀏覽器也會向其他公司取得內容；如同所有網站，它們會收到你的 IP 位址：Google（地圖、餐廳照片與網站字型）、ExchangeRate-API（open.er-api.com，在你切換幣別時提供當日匯率），以及僅在你登入時使用的 jsDelivr（登入程式）。

你的位置

當你點選「我附近」或「使用我的位置」時，瀏覽器會詢問是否分享你的位置。若你允許，與餐廳之間的距離會在你的裝置上計算；你的位置絕不會傳送給我們，也不會記錄在瀏覽統計中。若你改為在「我附近」頁面輸入城鎮或郵遞區號，會透過 Google 的地點搜尋查詢。若你查詢交通時間或沿途的餐廳，出發地與目的地會傳送給 Google 計算。只有在你勾選「記住這個位置」時才會保存，而且只存在你自己的瀏覽器中，連同附近餐廳的星級，以便下次顯示自上次造訪後新獲星的餐廳；取消勾選即會一併刪除。

Cookie 與 Google Analytics

如果你在 Cookie 橫幅上點選「接受」，我們也會使用 Google Analytics。它會設定 Cookie 以辨識回訪的訪客，讓我們了解網站的使用情形：瀏覽的頁面、停留時間、訪客來源，以及與 Umami 相同的匿名點擊次數。Google 依其自身的隱私權政策處理這些資料。如果你點選「拒絕」或未作選擇，Google Analytics 不會執行，也不會設定任何 Cookie。

瀏覽統計

我們使用 Umami 計算瀏覽量。Umami 不使用 Cookie，也不會識別你的身分。它記錄瀏覽的頁面、你從哪個網站而來、所在國家與裝置類型，以及匿名的點擊次數，例如收藏餐廳、儲存日記（絕不記錄內容）、分享頁面、搜尋或開啟餐廳網站。在「幫我挑選」頁面上，它會計算所選的答案，例如城市、餐點或預算，但絕不記錄你的位置。有人建立帳戶或登入時，只記錄這件事發生過，以及是使用電子郵件還是 Google，絕不記錄你的電子郵件地址。會員儲存偏好設定時，只記錄是哪一類（家鄉城市、幣別或飲食需求），絕不記錄所選的內容。會員開啟或關閉星級郵件、或取消訂閱時，只記錄這件事與郵件類型。星級郵件中的連結帶有標記，表示來自這類郵件，讓我們能計算它們帶來的瀏覽量，但絕不記錄是誰開啟的。

建立免費帳戶後

建立帳戶後，我們會保存：

  • 你的電子郵件地址；若你使用 Google 登入，也包括你 Google 帳戶上的名稱與大頭貼
  • 你願望清單上的餐廳、你標示為去過的餐廳，以及你填寫的日期
  • 你的偏好設定，僅在你於「你的帳戶」中設定，或在我們詢問是否記住某個選擇時同意才會保存：你的家鄉城市（本網站上的某個目的地，例如倫敦，絕不是你的地址或位置）、你想用來顯示價格的幣別，以及你的飲食需求
  • 你在用餐日記裡寫下的內容：每人花費與貨幣、你吃的菜單，以及你為任何收藏餐廳寫的筆記
  • 若你在「你的帳戶」中訂閱星級郵件：你選擇了哪些（「你附近的新星」，以及你想收到頒獎之夜摘要的國家）、讓每封郵件中的取消訂閱連結生效的隨機代碼，以及我們已寄給你哪些這類郵件的紀錄，確保你不會重複收到

清真、猶太潔食或無麩質等飲食需求可能透露你的宗教或健康資訊。我們只因你選擇儲存才會保存，只用來在「幫我挑選」中預先選好該選項，你也可以隨時在「你的帳戶」中清除。

你的日記是私人的。其他人都看不到；未經你同意，我們不會把它用於任何用途，包括核對我們的價格。

這些資料只用來讓你登入、在各裝置上顯示你的清單，讓每個頁面依你的偏好設定開啟，以及寄送你所訂閱的星級郵件。我們絕不出售這些資料，也不會為行銷目的分享；除非你要求，我們不會寄送電子報。

如果你寄信給我們

我們會保存你的電子郵件地址與來信內容以便回覆，不再需要時即會刪除（通常在一年內）。

我們使用資料的法律依據

  • 你的帳戶：為提供你所要求的帳戶（與你之間的契約）。
  • 你的飲食需求：你在選擇儲存時給予的明確同意；你可隨時清除以撤回同意。
  • 星級郵件：你在開啟時給予的同意；你可隨時使用任一封郵件中的取消訂閱連結或在「你的帳戶」中撤回。
  • Google Analytics：你的同意；你可隨時使用上方的「更改 Cookie 選擇」按鈕撤回。
  • Umami 瀏覽統計與回覆來信：我們經營與改善網站的正當利益，且方式不會識別你的身分，或由你主動發起。

由誰處理

  • Supabase：保存帳戶、清單與偏好設定，伺服器位於倫敦。
  • Resend：寄送登入郵件，以及你所訂閱的星級郵件。
  • Google：僅在你選擇使用 Google 繼續時。
  • Umami：計算瀏覽量，不使用 Cookie（見「瀏覽統計」）。
  • Google Analytics：僅在你接受 Cookie 時。

你的瀏覽器會保存一個登入憑證，讓你保持登入。登出時會刪除它，以及該裝置上的清單、日記與偏好設定副本。

保存多久

直到你刪除帳戶為止。你可以隨時在你的帳戶頁面刪除帳戶，帳戶、兩份清單、你的日記與偏好設定會立即移除。

你的權利

依英國資料保護法，你可以查看我們保存的資料、取得副本以帶往他處（在「你的帳戶」使用「下載我的資料」）、更正、刪除、反對資料的使用方式，或撤回你的同意。如有任何疑問，請寄信至 hello@starredbill.com，我們會在一個月內回覆。若對我們的回覆不滿意，可向英國資訊專員辦公室（ico.org.uk）投訴。
