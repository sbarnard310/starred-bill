Add it to your email

Do these on a computer. Copy first, then follow the steps for the email program you use. The little receipt logo is loaded from starredbill.com, so it shows for everyone you write to.

Gmail (in a web browser)
  1. On this page, press Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature. Click Create new and call it Starred Bill.
  4. Click inside the big box on the right and press ⌘ V (Ctrl V on Windows).
  5. Under Signature defaults, pick Starred Bill for For new emails use. For replies, either pick the same one, or copy the shorter version here as a second signature called Starred Bill reply and pick that for On reply/forward use.
  6. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes.
Apple Mail (on a Mac)
  1. On this page, press Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, open the Mail menu, then Settings, then the Signatures tab.
  3. Click your starredbill.com account on the left, then the + button under the middle list. Name it Starred Bill.
  4. Untick Always match my default message font.
  5. Click in the box on the right, select the text Mail put there and press ⌘ V.
  6. At the bottom, set Choose Signature to Starred Bill, then close the window. It saves by itself.

If the logo shows as a small blue question mark, that's Mail waiting to load it. It appears in emails you send.

Outlook
  1. On this page, press Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, open Settings (the gear), then Accounts, then Signatures.
  3. Click New signature, name it Starred Bill, click in the box and press ⌘ V (Ctrl V on Windows).
  4. Choose it for new messages and replies/forwards, then click Save.
On your phone
  1. The Gmail app only takes plain text. In the app, tap your picture, then Gmail settings, your account, Signature settings (or Mobile signature), and type the short version, for example: Sam Barnard · The Starred Bill · starredbill.com
  2. On an iPhone's own Mail app: Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature, and type the same line.
