Method

How the prices are counted

Per person, food only

Every figure is the menu price for one guest. Wine pairings, soft-drink pairings and supplements such as caviar or truffle courses are left out.

Before service charge

Most London restaurants add a discretionary 12.5–15% service charge. We show the menu price so restaurants compare on the same basis.

Lunch versus dinner

Dinner is the full tasting menu, or a typical three-course spend where there isn't one. Lunch is the set lunch, shown only where the restaurant offers one.
