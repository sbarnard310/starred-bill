    Method

    How the prices are counted

    Per person, before service

    Every figure is for one guest and excludes service charge, which most London restaurants add at 12.5–15%. Supplements such as caviar or truffle courses are left out.

    Dinner and wine

    Dinner is the tasting menu where there is one. Where a restaurant is à la carte only, we show the average main course price, marked “per main”. Wine is the cheapest pairing on offer.

    Google rating

    The Google reviews score out of 5, taken when the prices were collected. It reflects diners' overall experience, so it's a useful check alongside the stars and the bill.
